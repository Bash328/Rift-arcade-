Multiplayer games that run in your browser

Rift Arcade is an arcade of 3D online games. There is nothing to download and no account to make: pick a cabinet, jump into a public room, or create a private room and send friends the 4-letter code or invite link. Play on a computer with the keyboard, or on a phone in landscape with the on-screen joystick and jump button.

Cube Tag

A fast online tag game for 2 to 8 players. One animal is IT; touch someone to pass it on. Every second you are not IT scores a point, and the highest score after 60 seconds wins. Jump onto low platforms and chain them to reach the tall blocks, where nobody on the ground can tag you.

Voidfall

A survival game for 2 to 12 players. Every tile cracks the moment you land on it and drops away a second later, so you have to keep moving and hop across the gaps. The last player standing wins.
