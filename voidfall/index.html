HOW TO PLAY

Every tile cracks the moment you land on it and drops away a second later, so keep moving. Steer with WASD, the arrow keys, or the joystick, and hop across gaps with SPACE or the JUMP button. Last player standing wins. To play with friends, create a private room and tap the ROOM code to share the invite link, or tell them the 4-letter code.
