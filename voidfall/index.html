HOW TO PLAY

Every tile cracks the moment you land on it and drops away, so keep moving. Rounds take turns between two modes: classic Voidfall (one floor, every tile drops a second after you land) and Hex-A-Gone (three floors of hexagons where each tile breaks at its own speed: green lasts, yellow is quicker, red goes almost at once, and a tile that is cracking flashes white; fall through a gap and you land on the floor below, or you are out if there is no tile there; the floors above you disappear once you have dropped, so you can see where you are going). Steer with WASD, the arrow keys, or the joystick, and hop across gaps with SPACE or the JUMP button. Last player standing wins. To play with friends, create a private room and tap the ROOM code to share the invite link, or tell them the 4-letter code.
