READY PLAYER?

Want true full screen? Your browser keeps its address bar in games, but you can add Rift Arcade to your home screen and open it from there with nothing around it.

iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
Android: open the browser menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.

HOW TO PLAY

Steer with WASD, the arrow keys, or drag the joystick, and jump with SPACE or the JUMP button. The big glowing red cube is IT: touch someone to pass it on. Low steps and walls can be jumped onto; chain them to reach the tall blocks, where nobody on the ground can tag you. Every second you are not IT scores a point, and the highest score after 60 seconds wins. To play with friends, create a private room and tap the ROOM code to share the invite link, or tell them the 4-letter code.
